BRAND IDENTITY SUMMARY · SEPTEMBER 2026

A weekend walking club for women in their twenties and thirties in New York. Members apply, get approved by hand, and receive the meet point twenty-four hours before the walk. Two easy miles, dogs optional, finishing at a different West Village coffee shop each time.

Positioning

Sporty, vintage, cool-girl. The reference point is the American athletic club of the seventies and eighties — collegiate patches, single-ink sweatshirt prints, faded twill — read through a wellness lens rather than a competitive one. Nothing about pace, distance goals, or performance.

Safety is a design principle, not a feature. Manual approval, a required Instagram handle, and a meet point that only unlocks for approved members are the three mechanics carrying it.

Name and wordmark

The primary lockup stacks three lowercase lines — walkers / wellness club / new york — with a horizontal stripe field filling the space to the right of each line and a running dog breaking out of the last one. A serif line sits underneath: Est 2026 · Dog Walk Social Club.

A secondary oval patch exists for caps, stickers, and the app icon: heavy stacked type, striped ground, red border. It is deliberately more worn and more compact than the sweatshirt lockup.

App structure

SPLASHFull lockup, the premise in three sentences, request to join.
APPLICATIONName, Instagram handle, dog or no dog, dog's name and breed and size, why you want to walk.
PENDINGThree-step explanation of what happens next. Sets the expectation of hand review.
WALKSThis Saturday's walk, headcount and dog count, locked or unlocked meet point, the merch note, the coffee stop, house rules.
RECAPSPhotos from last Saturday plus short member notes.
SHOPFour pieces, all marked coming soon. A note that walk-box merch is not for sale.
YOUMember card, walks and miles and cafés, a full dog profile, bring-a-friend.

Merch mechanic

A box of club pieces comes to every walk and the earliest arrivals take one home. No counter, no claim ticket, no countdown — the app states it plainly and stops there. Scarcity does the work; the app doesn't gamify it.

Open items

  • The running dog is a geometric placeholder built from ellipses and bars. It needs a proper vector redraw — side profile, mid-stride, curled tail — before anything goes to print or embroidery.
  • Photography direction is unset. Every image in the app is a striped placeholder.
  • The oval patch still uses a royal blue closer to the vintage reference than to the club blue. Pick one.
  • Apparel and accessory designs are in progress separately.
  • No decision yet on how a member brings a guest, or how reporting is handled after a walk.
